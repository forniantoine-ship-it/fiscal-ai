# Landing Page Rules

## Page Goal

The landing page must immediately communicate:

"This is the simplest way to prepare a LMNP declaration."

The page should reduce:
- accounting anxiety,
- perceived complexity,
- fear of fiscal administration.

The product should feel:
- modern,
- premium,
- simple,
- reassuring,
- highly automated.

The landing page should communicate:

"The software does the complex work for me."

---

## UX Intent

The experience should feel:
- calm,
- elegant,
- highly understandable,
- conversion-oriented without aggressive marketing.

Users visiting the page are usually:
- overwhelmed by LMNP complexity,
- frustrated by existing software,
- comparing accountants and online tools,
- looking for simplicity and reassurance.

The page should feel closer to:
- premium consumer software,
than to fiscal or accounting websites.

Avoid:
- accounting software aesthetics,
- fintech landing page clichés,
- startup AI overmarketing,
- aggressive sales tactics.

---

## Core Messaging

The core product promise is:

"Finally, a LMNP declaration experience that feels simple."

The product should communicate:
- AI-assisted automation,
- document-driven workflow,
- minimal user effort,
- modern experience,
- transparent pricing.

The landing page should constantly reinforce:
- simplicity,
- time saving,
- clarity,
- reassurance.

---

## Hero Section

The hero section is critical.

Layout:
- large heading,
- supporting text,
- primary CTA,
- product preview.

The product preview should display:
- the dashboard,
- workflow progression,
- minimal interface,
- premium visual design.

Avoid:
- generic SaaS illustrations,
- abstract AI graphics,
- fintech imagery.

---

### Hero Headline

The headline should feel:
- simple,
- strong,
- emotionally reassuring.

Recommended direction:

"Your LMNP declaration. Finally simple."

---

### Hero Supporting Text

The supporting text should explain:
- upload documents,
- AI processing,
- user validation,
- automatic declaration preparation.

Tone:
- calm,
- human,
- understandable by non-accountants.

---

### Hero CTA

Primary CTA:
"Start my declaration"

Secondary CTA:
"See demonstration"

CTA hierarchy should remain:
- calm,
- premium,
- non-aggressive.

---

### Hero Pricing Visibility

Pricing should be visible immediately.

Display:
149€ TTC

Include:
EDI transmission included.

The pricing visibility should reinforce:
- transparency,
- simplicity,
- better value than accountants.

---

## How It Works Section

This is one of the most important sections.

The goal is instant comprehension.

Users should immediately think:
"Okay, this actually looks simple."

The workflow should feel:
- obvious,
- document-driven,
- highly automated.

Suggested structure:
1. Upload documents
2. AI prepares the file
3. Validate information
4. Generate declaration

Avoid:
- technical accounting explanations,
- complex fiscal flows,
- feature overload.

---

## Product Demonstration Section

The landing page should visually demonstrate:
- dashboard,
- uploads,
- AI extraction,
- amortization workflow,
- validation flow.

The product itself is the main visual asset.

The interface should visually sell:
- simplicity,
- trust,
- premium quality.

---

## Differentiation Section

The product should subtly differentiate itself from:
- complicated LMNP software,
- expensive accountants,
- stressful accounting workflows.

The tone should remain:
- elegant,
- restrained,
- confident.

Avoid aggressive comparison marketing.

---

## AI Positioning

AI must remain:
- useful,
- discreet,
- practical,
- reassuring.

Avoid:
- futuristic AI branding,
- exaggerated AI claims,
- "AI revolution" messaging.

The AI should feel:
- invisible but intelligent.

---

## Pricing Section

The pricing section should remain:
- extremely simple,
- highly readable,
- premium.

Preferred structure:
- one main pricing card,
- yearly payment,
- transparent inclusions.

Display:
- declaration generation,
- amortization automation,
- EDI transmission,
- PDF generation.

Multiple properties should redirect toward:
- custom quote workflow.

Avoid:
- SaaS pricing complexity,
- monthly subscriptions,
- multiple confusing plans.

---

## FAQ Section

FAQ should reduce:
- uncertainty,
- fear,
- friction.

Important topics:
- accounting knowledge,
- required documents,
- data security,
- LMNP/LMP support,
- EDI transmission,
- yearly workflow continuity.

The FAQ should remain:
- lightweight,
- readable,
- reassuring.

---

## Trust & Security Section

Security messaging should feel:
- calm,
- premium,
- discreet.

Avoid:
- technical cybersecurity overload,
- fear-based messaging.

Focus on:
- secure uploads,
- encrypted documents,
- private data protection.

---

## Final CTA Section

The page should end with:
- strong breathing room,
- calm reassurance,
- simple CTA.

Recommended emotional direction:

"Your LMNP declaration has never felt this simple."

Final CTA:
"Start my declaration"

---

## Important Constraints

The landing page must never feel:
- overly corporate,
- aggressive,
- startup-hyped,
- technically overwhelming,
- accounting-heavy.

The page should prioritize:
- emotional reassurance,
- clarity,
- simplicity,
- premium perception,
- calm conversion.

The user should leave the page thinking:

"This looks much simpler than everything else I’ve seen."

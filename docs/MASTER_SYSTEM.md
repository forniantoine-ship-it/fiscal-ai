# FISCAL AI — MASTER SYSTEM

## Product Philosophy

Fiscal AI is not an accounting software.

The product must feel:

- simple,
- calm,
- guided,
- premium,
- almost invisible.

The user should never feel:

- overwhelmed,
- stressed,
- lost in accounting complexity,
- inside an ERP or admin software.

The core product philosophy is:

Upload → AI Processing → User Validation

The AI performs the complex accounting work silently in the background.

The user experience must always feel:

- effortless,
- fluid,
- reassuring,
- understandable by non-accountants.

The product is designed for:

- LMNP and LMP property owners,
- non-accounting users,
- people who want efficiency,
- people who do not want to spend hours understanding taxes.

The interface should constantly communicate:

"The software is doing the work for me."

The product must never look like:

- accounting software,
- fintech dashboard,
- startup AI toy,
- ERP interface,
- spreadsheet software.

The experience should feel closer to:

- Apple,
- Linear,
- ADN Family,

than to traditional accounting platforms.

## Business Rules

The product is designed primarily for:
- LMNP property owners,
- LMP property owners,
- non-accountant users.

The experience must remain:
- extremely simple,
- highly automated,
- guided,
- premium.

Core workflow:
Upload Documents → AI Processing → User Validation → Payment → Declaration Generation → EDI Transmission

The product must automate:
- fiscal data extraction,
- expense categorization,
- amortization calculations,
- yearly fiscal preparation,
- declaration generation.

The user should never manually perform:
- accounting calculations,
- amortization logic,
- complex fiscal categorization.

Pricing model:
- single yearly payment,
- 149€ TTC,
- EDI transmission included.

Avoid:
- monthly subscriptions,
- complex pricing tiers,
- SaaS-style pricing systems.

Multi-property behavior:
- one property is the standard workflow,
- multiple properties trigger a custom quote flow.

The product should support:
- LMNP,
- LMP.

The first version does not support:
- SCI IS,
- advanced corporate structures.

The declaration generation process should feel:
- immediate,
- automatic,
- seamless.

No human validation step is visible in the standard flow.

The product should always prioritize:
- simplicity,
- automation,
- reassurance,
over accounting transparency.

Users should feel:
- the software understands their fiscal situation,
- the software builds the declaration automatically,
- they remain in control through validation.

The dashboard acts as:
- onboarding,
- progress tracker,
- central navigation hub.

The validation page is the premium conversion step.

The validation experience should:
- demonstrate the value created by the AI,
- reassure the user,
- naturally lead to payment.

The payment flow must remain:
- simple,
- elegant,
- non-aggressive.

The product should constantly communicate:
"This is easier and more modern than traditional accounting workflows."

The long-term product vision includes:
- yearly declaration continuity,
- amortization history,
- recurring expense memory,
- future automated imports,
- future fiscal continuity between years.
